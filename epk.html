Bio

Knuckz Young is a Richmond, Virginia rapper, producer, and all-around entertainer. Raised in Glen Allen by a mother who held the family down, he came up the way a lot of artists do, with the streets as his first teacher. He has survived being shot three times and spent years in the life before deciding to change course.

Today he’s a husband, a father of four, and a working man who turns that hard-earned perspective into honest, soulful music. A fixture on the Richmond scene, he has performed at the RVA Rap Elite Cyphers, The National, The Top (formerly The Kingdom), Satellite, and venues across the city.

His latest album, Empire, is out now.
